01

Polysaccharide Conformations & Molecular Recognition多糖构象与分子识别

I investigate how β-(1→3)-glucan structure controls its behavior in water and its recognition by proteins. All-atom simulations, enhanced sampling and free-energy analysis connect chain flexibility and hydration to single- and triple-helical conformations. Molecular docking and binding-energy analysis then resolve how ordered glucan surfaces interact with Dectin-1. Related studies examine how sulfation and phosphorylation alter hydrogen-bond networks, helical stability and possible recognition interfaces, providing molecular explanations for structure-dependent interactions.我研究β-(1→3)-葡聚糖的结构如何影响其水溶液行为及蛋白识别。通过全原子模拟、增强采样和自由能分析，揭示链柔性、水合与单链及三螺旋构象之间的联系；结合分子对接和结合能分析，解析有序糖链表面与Dectin-1的相互作用。相关工作进一步考察硫酸化和磷酸化如何改变氢键网络、螺旋稳定性及潜在识别界面，从分子层面理解结构依赖的识别行为。

02

Shape-Controlled & Responsive Colloidal Assembly形状调控与可响应胶体自组装

I study how particle shape and directional interactions select colloidal structures. In photoresponsive hybrid colloids, simulations and experiments connect particle geometry to hexagonal, distorted hexagonal and square lattices, while stronger light-induced attractions favor chain-like aggregates. Complementary work on faceted Janus particles explores how patch symmetry, steric constraints and depletion interactions produce finite clusters, including chiral assemblies built from achiral particles. These studies identify microscopic design variables for controlling colloidal organization.我研究颗粒形状与方向性相互作用如何选择胶体的组装结构。在光响应复合胶体中，模拟与实验共同揭示颗粒几何形状对六方、畸变六方和正方晶格的调控，而更强的光诱导吸引会促使体系形成链状聚集体。对于具有多面体斑块的Janus颗粒，相关工作考察斑块对称性、空间位阻和耗尽相互作用如何产生有限团簇，以及非手性颗粒如何组装成手性结构。

03

Colloidal Glasses & Structure–Dynamics Relations胶体玻璃化与结构—动力学关联

I examine how microscopic surface features reshape the collective dynamics of anisotropic colloids. Combining coarse-grained molecular dynamics with colloidal experiments, this work compares smooth and rough ellipsoids approaching the glass transition. Surface roughness couples translation and rotation, removing the intermediate orientational glass observed for smooth particles. The findings show that similar local orientational structures can exhibit different dynamics, highlighting the importance of detailed particle morphology in understanding glass formation.我关注微观表面结构如何改变各向异性胶体的集体动力学。通过粗粒化分子动力学与胶体实验相结合，比较光滑和粗糙椭球接近玻璃化转变时的行为。表面粗糙度使平移与转动发生耦合，消除了光滑颗粒体系中存在的中间取向玻璃态。研究表明，相似的局域取向结构可以对应不同的动力学行为，强调颗粒形貌细节在理解玻璃形成过程中的作用。

04

Pathways in Polymer Assembly & Disassembly聚合物组装与解组装路径

I use coarse-grained simulations to explore how the preparation history and response of amphiphilic block copolymers control their assembled structures. Converting hydrophobic segments into hydrophilic ones destabilizes preformed vesicles and opens pathways to toroidal, rod-like and spherical micelles. The final morphology depends on the extent and rate of conversion as well as the initial state. This work explains how controlled disassembly can access structures that are absent from direct self-assembly under the same simulated conditions.我利用粗粒化模拟研究制备历史与链段响应如何控制两亲性嵌段共聚物的组装结构。将疏水链段转化为亲水链段，可使预先形成的囊泡失稳，并沿不同路径演化为环状、棒状或球状胶束。最终形貌同时取决于转化程度、转化速度和初始状态。这项工作揭示，受控解组装能够获得在相同模拟条件下直接自组装难以形成的结构。
