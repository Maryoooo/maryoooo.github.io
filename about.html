Biography & background个人简介与学术经历

I am a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, where I work on computational soft matter in the School of Chemistry, Chemical Engineering and Biotechnology.我现为新加坡南洋理工大学化学、化工与生物技术学院博士后研究员，主要从事计算软物质研究。

I received my Ph.D. in Polymer Chemistry and Physics in 2022 through the Changchun Institute of Applied Chemistry, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and the University of Science and Technology of China. Before that, I studied Polymer Materials Science and Engineering at Ocean University of China.我于 2022 年完成中国科学院长春应用化学研究所与中国科学技术大学的高分子化学与物理博士培养，并于此前在中国海洋大学获得高分子材料与工程专业工学学士学位。
