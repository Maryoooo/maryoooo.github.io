Xuan Feng 冯玄

Research Fellow博士后研究员

School of Chemistry, Chemical Engineering and Biotechnology
Nanyang Technological University, Singapore化学、化工与生物技术学院
新加坡南洋理工大学

I use computer simulations to study how molecular interactions and particle geometry shape the organization and dynamics of soft matter. My work connects colloidal self-assembly and glass transitions with the conformations and molecular recognition of carbohydrate polymers.我利用计算机模拟研究分子相互作用与颗粒几何形状如何影响软物质的结构和动力学。研究涵盖胶体自组装与玻璃化转变，以及多糖的构象和分子识别机制。
